About

I am an undergraduate student at the University of California, Berkeley, studying Data Science and Neuroscience. I am interested in developing computational methods that make neural interfaces more reliable, efficient, and useful in real-world settings. My long-term goal is to contribute to technologies that help restore function and improve quality of life for people affected by neurological disorders or injuries.

Research Interests

Non-invasive BCI

Neural Signal Processing
