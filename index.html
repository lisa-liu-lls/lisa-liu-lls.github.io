I am an undergraduate student at UC Berkeley studying Data Science and Neuroscience. I focus on developing computational methods to make neural interfaces reliable, efficient, and applicable in real-world settings to restore function for individuals affected by neurological disorders or injuries.

Research Interests

My interests lie at the intersection of computational methods and neural data. The descriptions below are placeholders and will be updated as my research develops.

Machine Learning

Exploring machine learning methods to learn meaningful patterns from complex data, with an interest in robust models for neuroscience applications.

Time-series & Multimodal Data

Studying time-dependent signals and how information from multiple modalities, such as EEG and fNIRS, can be integrated and interpreted.

Signal Processing

Interested in filtering, artifact removal, feature extraction, and improving the quality of noisy physiological recordings.

Non-invasive BCI

Exploring EEG- and fNIRS-based brain–computer interfaces, particularly neural decoding and computational approaches to accessible BCI systems.
